🌿 Research Projects

🧬 Unraveling the Complex Dynamics of the Human Microbiome

I am currently engaged in studying the intricate interactions within the human microbiome, focusing on the skin and oral microbiomes. These microbial communities play essential roles in maintaining health and influencing diseases like atopic dermatitis (AD).

My research delves into how shifts in skin microbial composition contribute to AD's onset and flare-ups, with a special focus on microbial dysbiosis and immune system modulation. I also study the impact of environmental toxins—such as air pollutants and heavy metals—on these microbes.

Furthermore, I explore how lifestyle factors, especially e-cigarette smoking, disrupt the oral microbiome, potentially causing inflammation and systemic health effects. My goal is to identify early microbial and functional markers of environmental exposure to support personalized interventions.

🔬 Metabolomic Insights into Microbiome-Mediated Health Benefits

This project investigates how microbial metabolites influence human physiology, especially immune regulation, skin barrier function, and inflammation—key aspects in atopic dermatitis.

I study how pollutants such as microplastics and POPs alter microbial metabolism, potentially leading to harmful, pro-inflammatory compounds. The research looks not only at single metabolites but at the broader metabolic pathways they influence.

The ultimate goal is to define metabolic signatures tied to environmental exposures and identify probiotic or dietary strategies that promote metabolic balance and reduce disease risk.

💻 Computational Integration of Omics Data

To capture the full picture of microbiome-environment-health interactions, I develop advanced computational pipelines that integrate multi-omics data: metagenomics, transcriptomics, metabolomics, and more.

My models use machine learning, network analysis, and statistical frameworks to uncover patterns linking environmental toxins to disease outcomes, such as in atopic dermatitis.

I also build GPU-accelerated platforms for interactive omics data visualization—accelerating the discovery of key biomarkers and enabling predictive modeling for early diagnosis and personalized treatments.
